1. ASSIGNMENT AND PRODUCT DIRECTION

Work in the existing srkftw/lemma repository. Establish the architecture and implement the first working foundation of Lemma School and Lemma Cloud. Continue through implementation, integration, meaningful verification, and a reviewable handoff. A proposal, directory scaffold, collection of interfaces, attractive disconnected dashboard, or roadmap alone does not complete this assignment.

Lemma is an open educational and research system for learning, teaching, operating schools, and developing the Lemma hardware/software suite. Its maintained application and system logic is written in Lean. LemmaSim and the eventual Lemma One are clients of the same school and device contracts. A school can run on its owner's machines, use its own curriculum and tutor configuration, export its work, and operate without a mandatory Lemma-hosted account, proprietary model API, or hyperscaler service.

The central distributable object is a versioned school package. It composes source references, curriculum definitions, tutor policy, learner-model configuration, model artifacts, independent assessments, permissions, and deployment requirements. Learner records, private recordings, credentials, and a running deployment's identity are separate from that reusable package.

The same person may study, teach, administer a school, or develop Lemma. These are activities and scoped capabilities, not permanent account castes. Everyone can create their own workspace, inspect the system, author material, experiment, and fork permitted packages. Authority over another person's records or a shared school's operation remains explicitly scoped.

USGCE is the first reference school using these public interfaces. Its only inherited curriculum authority is the owner's attached textbook collection and topic catalogue/course outline. Do not make USGCE a special privileged tenant or bake its content, naming, educational assumptions, or model choices into the platform.
