SG13G2 simple-enclosure preflight1

lemma-sg13g2-enclosure-inputs/1 pins the complete 34-file input closure in tooling/silicon/enclosure-inputs-v1.json. The host-only scripts/silicon_enclosure.py produces a candidate technology LEF under an unused output directory. It does not replace the PDK or the maintained layout flow. IHP SG13G2 remains provisional.

The admitted subset is the original ten unconditioned, two-overhang ENCLOSURE clauses on seven cut layers, including their ABOVE/BELOW distinctions. Integer nanometre rectangles and exact rectangle-union coverage test both allowed overhang orientations. Every fixed cut and all eight rigid orthogonal orientations are checked. Generated-via minimum enclosures and each cut in a 2-by-3 array are checked separately. Bounding-box coverage cannot hide holes.

The original standard-cell GDS, cell abstract cut shapes, fixed vias and via generators determine the cut classes. GDS admission is limited to the pinned units encoding, rectangular cut boundaries and unscaled orthogonal references. Unknown inputs, cut shapes, non-square classes and unsupported enclosure clauses fail. This is a closed corpus check, not a general GDS/LEF DRC engine.

The source Via1_DC2L Metal1 rectangle extends only 5 nm left of its first Via1 cut; the original BELOW rule requires 10/50 nm. Without --repair-known-via, generation fails. The explicit repair changes only that Metal1 minimum X from -100 to -105 nm in the copied LEF. The source PDK remains untouched. Independent Magic fixtures require the original geometry to report the enclosure violation and the repaired geometry to clear it under the same pinned process deck. Neither fixture qualifies a complete controller.

Every original ENCLOSURE clause is retained. Measured square CUTCLASS definitions and duplicates of those same enclosure bounds are added as LEF58 properties. Actual OpenROAD/OpenDB import must report all seven classes, ten qualified rules and ten retained original rules with exact bounds and side flags. Input hashes are checked again after tool execution. OpenDB admission establishes parsing; it does not establish TritonRoute enforcement.

Tool children have private process groups, bounded waits and owned termination and reaping on failure. The CLI limits address space to 4 GiB, individual files to 1 GiB and each tool wait to 15–300 seconds. Outputs and failures remain in their original directories. A failed check never returns an ordinary success.

Actual router enforcement, fixed/generated/routed-pin geometry, all other rule classes, complete process DRC, LVS, extracted parasitics, post-route timing and full-chip closure remain separate. Existing unsupported legacy-rule warnings are retained; neither the candidate nor the isolated via repair assigns the cause of earlier routing timeouts.
