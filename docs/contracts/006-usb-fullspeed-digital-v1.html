USB full-speed digital PHY v1

The additive lemma-silicon-dev/0.11.0 binding uses descriptor ABI 12, features 15, Kernel 6, Power 2, Paper 3 and the existing USB aperture 0x10010a00/0x400/PLIC 16. Profiles 0.6/0.8 retain their decoded-packet boundary. The packet controller remains version 1; PHY abort handling is enabled only for the new binding. This contract adds digital circuitry, not electrical USB qualification or complete USB2 support.

The primary reference is the USB-IF USB 2 specification, base PDF SHA256 d39698a33486c399124af92bd02e4f978fd9a836b5cf4e52e6e4633eb1d89f61. The pinned public archive was usb_20_20250603.zip, SHA256 5fe9c53c04033818af396e8852b3acbca5c3a76ba92fab549fd81cd0ea7b3692. Sections 7.1.8–7.1.11 define the encoding, stuffing, SYNC and full-speed rate; 7.1.7.5 covers reset and 8.3.5 the CRCs. Vendor files remain ignored research inputs; this repository does not redistribute them.

lemma_usb_fs_phy.sv evaluates on the declared 100 MHz clock. Its registered phase accumulator generates exactly 12 sampling enables per 25 clocks, with four samples per 12 MHz symbol. Hard-decision lines are {D-,D+}: J=01, K=10, SE0=00, SE1=11. No voltage, rise time, cable or analog receiver is modeled. The receiver retimes on differential transitions; no host code decodes NRZI or substitutes a packet-success flag.

This 100 MHz lattice emits 80/90 ns symbol periods. Its mean rate is 12 MHz, but it does not satisfy the complete USB source-edge timing obligation. Section 7.1.13.1.1 allows 2 ns variation for consecutive differential transitions and 1 ns for paired transitions; 10 ns edge quantization cannot establish those bounds. The independent waveform oracle uses its predeclared one-system-clock quantization bound, separately from the normative jitter envelope. A finer clock domain with declared CDC and checkpoint semantics is required for that digital timing gate. This gap is separate from analog and electrical qualification.

The transmitter streams at most 67 bytes including PID/CRC. It generates eight-symbol SYNC, NRZI, a stuffed zero after six ones including the final SYNC one, any required final stuffed zero, two SE0 symbols and one J symbol. It never stretches a physical bit for source starvation: underflow emits an invalid SE1/EOP and cannot count as a transmitted packet. A two-symbol guard prevents immediate reuse of the transmitter.

The receiver rejects malformed SYNC, SE1, seven consecutive ones, excess capacity, malformed EOP and byte alignment. It tolerates the admitted bounded last-bit dribble. Rejected physical packets remain quarantined until EOP or an observed SE0 followed by three idle J symbols; it cannot seek another SYNC inside the rejected packet. A short malformed EOP therefore cannot poison the next valid transaction indefinitely. A complete output held by backpressure retains its byte/last/valid. Streaming overflow aborts partial staging rather than publishing a shortened packet. The controller clears only its partial packet/token state on a PHY abort; already published endpoint heads survive.

The SoC contains two independent registered PHY instances. Hosted Lean still produces the existing raw packet commands; the host PHY encodes them onto a resolved digital wire and the chip PHY decodes them into the actual controller. Controller output follows the reverse route. Equal simultaneous drives retain their digital value; conflicting drives produce SE1. A modeled root reset drives SE0. PHY reset qualification begins after 250 continuous system clocks; the preserved controller/root policy still uses its declared 10 ms/50 ms reset budgets. Host process transport never writes addressed/configured state.

The raw SENT result means the host PHY has completed EOP/release and its guard, rather than merely having accepted a byte buffer. It is no durable receipt. The existing guest Store journal and independently persistent Lean peer still determine Save acknowledgment through a backing barrier.

Standard transaction timing is unfinished. The development packet controller retains 100 us token/ACK timeouts and the raw process adapter polls at 1000-clock boundaries. A standards-timed host needs autonomous token/data batching and ACK sequencing at physical packet boundaries. Host wall time is not virtual USB timing. SOF scheduling, suspend/resume/remote wake, hubs, isochronous endpoints, high-/low-speed operation, electrical behavior and full USB conformance are separate missing work. No allocated VID/PID or certification is claimed.

PHY phases, buffer contents, cursors, bit/stuff counters, retained outputs, quarantine, guard and reset counters are generated-model authoritative state. The profile 11 container is version 8. It additionally admits a logical SEND whose bytes have all entered the host PHY while physical TX remains busy; restoring an already completed retained SEND rejects before adoption. Existing profile container formats remain unchanged. A restored attached USB fork is suspended until an implementation-bound captured outcome tape covers its exact accepted prefix and future horizon. It never contacts/resubmits to the live peer. Snapshot/tape export does not poll, clock, drain or mutate the live controller. Circuit suffix qualification and whole-machine pending suffix qualification remain distinct; QEMU's cache-continuation gap is unaffected.
