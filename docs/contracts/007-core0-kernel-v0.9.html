core0-kernel-v0.9

Additive source profile for the explicit Multimodal14 deployment. Inherits the same genuine elaboration, scalar arithmetic, bounded Action, device fences and owned physical-memory semantics as Kernel4. It adds aligned 64-bit device accesses only in [0x10014000,0x10016000), and aligned 32-bit PLIC priority19 at [0x0c00004c,0x0c000050). It grants no BootNV, CommonOFDM, USB or arbitrary device address. Historical profiles keep their previous bounds.

No new Lean syntax, foreign runtime, CSR selector or managed capacity is admitted. The compiler emits structured hardware primitives only. The normal supervisor startup has an exclusive multimodal selector. Existing PMP entries0–7 retain their bytes and meaning; PMP8 is NAPOT0x10014000/8192 bytes, RW/NX, unlocked. pmpaddr8 and pmpcfg2 readback mismatch reaches machine fatal. Actual readbacks are stored at 0x804e01f0/+8 and validated by compiled S policy as 0x040053ff/0x1b before extension access. Product, CommonOFDM, non-LemmaSim and bootstrap combinations reject.

Guest-visible MMIO discovery uses a new versioned extension header and mandatory DTB child; the old 32-word/256-byte descriptor remains unchanged. Device policy, grants and lifecycle remain compiled Lean. The new deployment retains the isolated Store, Notebook, Arithmetic and Course roles; its numerical checker remains bounded exact rational/degree-two polynomial checking, not a Lean proof kernel.

Evidence is separate: current checkpoint. Elaboration or emitted ELF alone does not establish execution, digital parity, timing, physical safety or the whole multimodal specification.
