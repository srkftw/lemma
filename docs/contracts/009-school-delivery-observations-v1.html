School delivery observations1

The protected delivery-observe operation records bounded observations of an immutable authored decision. It derives the original school, learner, session, publication, policy, generation, ASK, document revision/object and package artifact from that decision. The verified admission principal must own it. A caller cannot retarget an observation to a new edition or learner. These are reports by a live transport observer, not device attestation, mathematical assessment or evidence of understanding.

The request fields are decision, exact ASK sequence, a fresh live-execution observer identity, kind and observation. Lean normalizes typed fields and derives the observation SHA256 from the complete original context and payload. An exact retry, including a lost acknowledgement or later authority generation, returns the original commitSequence with reused=true. Changed observations append distinct records. They never update the original decision. Typed observations use the existing delivery history kind; guest submission snapshots matching original decision/revision reports into its immutable attempt, and authorized teacher review returns that history beside the original artifact. Credential revocation is checked before admission; recording historical facts extends no session or worker authority.

All integers retain64-bit bounds. Unknown nested fields, including invented visibility flags, reject. The response contains observation SHA256, commitSequence, kind, sequence, reused and an explicit scope. The older delivery operation remains a legacy diagnostic report and is not upgraded to this typed contract.

Ordinary native and browser transports enqueue these reports away from machine execution. Browser transport captures the original peer, keeps at most64 pending and64 failed reports, and exposes explicit retry/status. Reconnection cannot retarget old receipts. The browser observes at most a busy/idle pair after a matching725. Credentials and receipt queues are live external state and are not serialized into machine checkpoints. Unacknowledged live receipt loss across process termination remains distinct from durable acknowledged school observations; guest submission durability is the separate envelope/queue contract.

Hosted protected HTTP tests, browser byte-transport fault tests, actual native/browser guest execution and physical presentation have separate evidence scopes. No observation here establishes sensor safety, educational benefit or whole-body visibility.

Attempt consumers match the current immutable invitation and the declared work context before attaching assistance. The ASK guest session, object, document revision, source, edition and task must match the submitted workspace. The decision must follow the current binding record and retain its exact school, learner, school session, publication, policy, generation and complete original ASK, including consent and sequence. Replacing a named session, restarting an authority, or reusing a revision number on another page cannot attach the older decision or delivery to new work. Historical observations stay immutable and remain available in their original context. The exposure flag remains conservative: a decision alone still does not prove visible presentation.
