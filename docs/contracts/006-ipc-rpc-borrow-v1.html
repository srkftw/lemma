Same-Action Receive/Reply descriptor reuse, version 1

lemma-ipc-rpc-base-reuse/1 preserves IPC-v1 packet limits, copied bytes, transactions, one-use reply authority and error precedence. The trusted IPC.dispatchWithLimit uses receiveWithLimit and the new replyLocal path. Historical public receive, reply and dispatch retain their original bodies. There is no new User pointer, cached authority, heap or syscall ABI.

Each new borrowed base comes from a successful full idle or checkedTransaction validation in that same nonpreemptible Supervisor Action. The descriptor identity, root, layout, leaf registry, frame generations, ownership and pins read by AddressSpace.base remain immutable through this Action. No mapping, frame allocation/free, descriptor replacement, task switch or suspension may intervene. No alias survives the call. The single hart runs no other User task during the Action. Interrupt handlers do not change these protected fields, and admitted controller DMA buffers cannot alias them.

Reply capability grants and revocation may change their own disjoint capability rows and object/queue state. They do not change the protected address-space base closure. Their original complete capability/object checks still execute; no grant, ownership or generation result is reused. pendingAt re-reads every live transaction field. Each UserCopy preflights the owned scratch handle and both user-page permissions afresh. Its existing aligned-word and byte-tail paths are unchanged. Zero-before-publication, pinning, quotas, expired-head withdrawal, namespace authority, partial-copy failures and cleanup are preserved.

The closed pre-LLVM observer qualifies sixteen actual emitted function bodies, their signatures/effects, all helper callers, and each base's exact derivation from the current successful metadata result. The older CallBegin guard admits these additional callers only after that full guard passes. Unknown callers, changed effects, stale metadata, wrong descriptors and missing functions reject.

The finite RAM fixture compares actual maintained old/new Actions for both 512 and 8192 frame profiles. Whole memory, results and the ordered remaining request/status/byte trace must match. Only identical successful duplicate AddressSpace.base read subsequences may be projected away. Faults at every distinct reached address retain their first applicable failure and prior writes. This fixture models stable protected RAM: it does not establish equivalence for transient errors on a later duplicate read, asynchronous descriptor mutation or unimplemented ECC. Those states are not admitted by this optimization contract.

In the 8192-frame fixture, a 256-byte call packet and 128-byte reply reduce Receive requests 3185 to 2711 and Reply requests 3347 to 3031. These are host observations of Lean Actions, not RTL cycles, fast-backend timing parity or a completed cold course load. The original 100-million-cycle cold Load failures remain failures. The private 2-million-cycle retained-boundary diagnostic is a PC sample distribution only: Frames/AddressSpace samples may also be transitive copy work, so direct copy-function counts do not prove causal dominance.
