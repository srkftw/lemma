Core0 kernel v0.3: LemmaSim device boundary

This additive kernel-only profile preserves all older source-language profiles. It admits exactly four new checked scalar operations: lemmasim.device-read32, device-write32, device-read64, and device-write64. Each elaborates a real Lean Action declaration. Loads/stores are volatile, little endian, require natural alignment and a whole-width in-range access, and have an I/O fence before and after the access. Writes reject values outside the selected register width.

32-bit ranges: modern VirtIO-MMIO registers [0x10001000,0x10001200), PLIC priorities [0x0c000004,0x0c000038), supervisor-context enables [0x0c002080,0x0c002084), and context1 threshold/claim-complete [0x0c201000,0x0c201008). 64-bit ranges: project paper and pen windows [0x10010000,0x10012000). These are a separate device catalog; ordinary physical RAM operations do not grant MMIO authority. User-v0.1 cannot express these operations. Kernel-v0.1/v0.2 reject them even when externally forged into an IR.

Only the explicit LemmaSim configuration selects v0.3 and its extra PMP/map boundary. LLVM20.1.8 RV64IM LP64 remains the backend and ABI. The profile extends neither managed Lean support nor arbitrary foreign-code linkage. Compiler fixture tests/lemmasim/CompilerDevices.lean checks genuine elaboration, rejection under old profiles, access widths, volatile operations, and fences. This local check is not compiler correctness or a whole-machine execution certificate.

The pinned optimizer may replace declared scalar arithmetic with llvm.usub.sat.i64 (unsigned subtraction clamped to zero) and llvm.fshl.i64 (concatenated unsigned funnel shift modulo 64). The compiled notebook's observed funnel shift has equal input words and therefore rotates its value. The artifact auditor admits these two optimizer intrinsics only for the closed LemmaSim configurations, together with historical min/max intrinsics. They lower to ordinary RV64IM integer instructions and introduce no runtime helper or target source-language operation. Undefined symbols, generated instructions, literal tables, ownership and stack accounting remain independently checked. This is accounting for pinned optimized artifacts, not a claim of general LLVM or Lean compiler correctness.
