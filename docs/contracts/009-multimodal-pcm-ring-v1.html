This additive digital contract is lemma-multimodal-converter/2. Integrated16 extends the complete Integrated15 address, interrupt, clock, reset, boot, SRAM pen and transport binding. Its controller header reports ABI2. A separately named Fast14 MM2 subset may execute the controller; it does not establish the Integrated16 boot, clocked USB or power union. ABI1 remains supported with its original reserved registers and 16-sample playback path. Hardware discovery is distinct from service negotiation and physical qualification.

At the existing 100 MHz controller aperture, global byte offsets 104 and 112 select an aligned base and a power-of-two capacity of 16–2048 signed 16-bit little-endian samples. The range must fit the existing owned DMA pool. Offset120 publishes a monotonic accepted producer position, 128 reads the fetched position, and 136 commands enable(1), EOF(2) or cancel(3). Offset144 reports unconfigured(0), active(1), EOF-complete(2), canceled(3) or fault(4); 152 reads the immutable EOF position, or UINT64_MAX before EOF. Offset160 counts real playback starvation gaps. Offset168 exposes held bus kind0/record-write1/audio-read2 and cancellation bit8. Reserved offset96 continues to report physical-qualified0.

One stable aligned 8-byte memory read transfers up to four samples into the existing 16-entry PCM FIFO. A final 1–3 sample tail requires explicit EOF; bytes beyond that tail are never submitted. The shared bus request retains direction, address, data and owner until acknowledgement. Completed record-write and audio-read beats alternate when both are eligible. Permission, lease, epoch and configuration are checked at read completion and playback. Cancellation or revocation stops playback immediately but retains a held read until its response; the response cannot alter a subsequent grant. The private ring frame cannot be reused while that read is held. Reset preserves its ordinary epoch and state semantics. Direct FIFO writes cannot mix with an enabled ring or an unretired ring read.

Accepted producer, fetched samples, actual FIFO submissions and digitally consumed samples are separate counters. Producer replay, capacity overrun, counter exhaustion and bus failures fail explicitly. EOF completion follows actual consumption without inventing a starvation event. Non-EOF starvation retains error10 and a gap count. None of these counters establishes an audible presentation or explanation delivery.

Lean kernel service negotiation reports version3 only on ABI2 and version2 on ABI1. Existing operations retain their identity. Stream8 operations19–22 require the endpoint capability. Operation19 allocates/binds a kernel-owned 4 KiB frame and accepts a 32-byte packet [magic0x32474e52, format1, capacity2048, reserved0]. It requires an armed stream and current playback grant. Operation20 accepts [magic0x32424350, format1, count1–512, expectedProducer] followed by exactly count S16_LE samples. It validates descriptor, grant and controller epochs, exact producer, available ring space and budget; copies into the private frame; checks permission again; then publishes the entire bounded packet or rejects it. It charges eight controller-budget bytes per accepted sample conservatively, not as proof of playback. It returns the new accepted producer.

Operation21 takes the expected producer as its scalar argument, seals EOF at that exact position and returns it. A repeated matching EOF is idempotent. Operation22 exposes raw observations selected by scalar0–9: status, accepted, fetched, submitted, consumed, gaps, held-bus state, controller error, controller epoch and capacity. The saved descriptor and grant epoch remain required for these historical observations after revocation; observations grant no new playback authority. A new owner cannot retarget the previous owner's query.

The maintained User consumer uses bounded packets through the same service and a real 4,099-sample arbitrary waveform at 16 kHz. Initial prefill precedes start; subsequent packets cross ring wrap; explicit EOF and actual counters determine completion. Legacy ABI1 uses the previous 16-sample test. Mute and revocation remain immediate.
