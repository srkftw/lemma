Selected 0.3 pen hardware reset and watchdog state

The selected fast successor resets the entire pen controller on completed hardware reset: FIFO and immutable head, epoch/sequence, loss/error/resync, last timestamp and contact deadline. Its declared sample maxima remain the same. Software RESET1, including cooperative suspend, still preserves the old head and enters loss/resynchronization. Those are distinct operations.

The pinned ACLINT resets MTIME to zero without resetting QEMU's local virtual clock. At 10 MHz its timer is floor(local_ns/100)-floor(reset_ns/100). The pen therefore retains a private 100 ns aligned reset origin, rather than subtracting the exact reset nanosecond. Mouse timestamps and watchdog deadlines use that origin. A large admitted u64 sample timestamp saturates the host timer at the signed virtual-clock limit; it cannot wrap into an immediate loss.

lemma-paper-pen/1 private VMState version 3 serializes this origin. Restore rejects version 2, misaligned/future/wrapped origins and inconsistent deadlines. The ordinary snapshot wrapper also binds the exact implementation and checks file bounds/checksums before applying a candidate. Every origin byte remains exact continuation state; no new checkpoint differences are permitted. Historical 0.1/0.2 derivations and binaries do not consume this extension.
