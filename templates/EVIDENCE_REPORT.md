## Exact claim

What does this evidence establish? What does it not establish?

## Inputs and versions

Source revision, dirty status, toolchain identities, flags, ABI/machine profile,
source/input hashes, dependencies and declared trust assumptions.

## Commands actually executed

Commands, exit codes, timeout outcomes and paths to raw logs. Separate observations
from proposed commands. Missing tools and blocked network access are not passing tests.

## Results

Accepted fixtures, negative fixtures and diagnostics, reference comparisons, optimized
versus unoptimized results, repeated clean builds, artifact audits and checksums.

## Remaining uncertainty

Unimplemented features, unsupported semantics, test limitations, assumptions and the
next smallest construction task. Do not mark the entire gate passed from one slice.
